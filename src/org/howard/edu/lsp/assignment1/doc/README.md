# Course Orientation

1. **Are late programming assignments accepted?**

   No. Late programming assignments are not accepted.

2. **Where will official course announcements be posted?**

   Official course announcements will be posted on Piazza.

3. **How will examination dates be announced?**

   Examination dates will be announced in class and posted on Piazza.

4. **Under what circumstances will a makeup examination be considered?**

   A makeup examination will be considered only for extraordinary circumstances beyond a student's control. The student must notify the instructor as soon as possible and provide the required documentation; documentation does not automatically guarantee a makeup examination.

5. **Is AI permitted in this course?**

   AI is permitted only when the instructor expressly authorizes it or an assignment states that it is allowed.

6. **Which course policy will have the greatest impact on your success this semester? Explain why (2–3 sentences).**

   The no-late-assignment policy will have the greatest impact on my success this semester. It means I must begin programming assignments early enough to test my code, resolve problems, and verify that every required file has been pushed to GitHub before the deadline.

7. **When are office hours for LSP Fall 2026?**

   Office hours are available by Zoom appointment on an as-needed basis.

8. **What is your responsibility for monitoring Piazza, and are you responsible for assignment updates, clarifications, or deadline changes posted there?**

   I am responsible for monitoring Piazza regularly. Yes, I am responsible for all assignment updates, clarifications, and deadline changes posted there, whether or not I receive a separate notification.

9. **What documentation is required if you are unable to take an examination because of a medical issue or other approved circumstance?**

   I must provide official documentation verifying that I was unable to take the examination at its scheduled time. For a medical issue, the documentation must state that I was medically unable to participate at that time; it does not need to disclose a diagnosis.

10. **Does scholarship status entitle a student to different grading, deadline, attendance, or course requirements?**

    No. Scholarship status does not entitle a student to different grading, deadline, attendance, or course requirements.

"I acknowledge that I have read the course syllabus and understand that I am responsible for complying with all course policies, assignment requirements, and monitoring Piazza throughout the semester."
